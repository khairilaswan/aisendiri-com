# Play Store listing text

Paste into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing. Add Bahasa Melayu (ms-MY) as the default language and English (en-US) as a translation. Character limits: name 30, short description 80, full description 4000.

## Bahasa Melayu (default)

**Nama aplikasi** (29)

AI Sendiri: AI Tanpa Internet

**Penerangan ringkas** (78)

AI peribadi dalam telefon anda. Sembang, cakap & tanya gambar, tanpa internet.

**Penerangan penuh**

AI Sendiri ialah pembantu AI yang berjalan terus dalam telefon anda. Muat turun AI sekali, kemudian gunakannya tanpa internet, tanpa akaun dan tanpa langganan.

Perbualan anda kekal dalam telefon. Mesej, suara dan gambar anda diproses dalam telefon dan tidak dihantar ke mana-mana pelayan.

APA YANG ANDA BOLEH BUAT
• Sembang dalam Bahasa Melayu, English atau campur-campur
• Bercakap dengan AI dan dengar jawapannya
• Tanya tentang gambar atau tangkapan skrin: resit, notis, carta, mesej
• Minta tolong tulis mesej, e-mel, ringkasan atau idea
• Guna di mana-mana: dalam kapal terbang, di kampung, atau bila data habis

DIBINA UNTUK MALAYSIA
• AI yang dipilih kerana faham Bahasa Melayu dan cara kita bercakap
• Aplikasi dalam Bahasa Melayu dan English

MUDAH UNTUK SEMUA
• Aplikasi menyemak telefon anda dan mencadangkan AI yang sesuai
• AI Ringan untuk telefon 4 GB ke atas, AI Pintar untuk telefon 8 GB ke atas
• Tetapan lanjutan disembunyikan, untuk mereka yang mahu

PRIVASI
• Tiada akaun, tiada iklan, tiada penjejakan
• Sembang disimpan dalam telefon sahaja, dan boleh dipadam bila-bila masa
• Internet hanya digunakan untuk memuat turun fail AI

PERLU TAHU
• Fail AI agak besar (0.7 hingga 4 GB). Muat turun guna Wi-Fi.
• AI boleh tersilap. Semak maklumat penting sebelum bergantung padanya.
• Laporkan jawapan yang tidak sesuai dengan butang Laporkan di bawah setiap jawapan.

## English

**App name** (30)

AI Sendiri: Offline Private AI

**Short description** (79)

A personal AI on your phone. Chat, talk and ask about photos, without internet.

**Full description**

AI Sendiri is an AI assistant that runs right on your phone. Download the AI once, then use it without internet, without an account and without a subscription.

Your conversations stay on your phone. Your messages, voice and photos are processed on the phone and are not sent to any server.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Chat in Bahasa Melayu, English or a mix of both
• Talk to the AI and hear it reply
• Ask about a photo or screenshot: receipts, notices, charts, messages
• Get help writing messages, emails, summaries or ideas
• Use it anywhere: on a plane, in the kampung, or when your data runs out

BUILT FOR MALAYSIA
• AIs chosen because they understand Bahasa Melayu and how we really talk
• App in Bahasa Melayu and English

EASY FOR EVERYONE
• The app checks your phone and suggests the right AI
• AI Ringan (Light) for 4 GB phones and up, AI Pintar (Smart) for 8 GB phones and up
• Advanced settings are tucked away for those who want them

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Chats are saved on your phone only, and you can delete them any time
• The internet is only used to download AI files

GOOD TO KNOW
• AI files are large (0.7 to 4 GB). Download over Wi-Fi.
• AI can make mistakes. Check important information before relying on it.
• Report unsuitable replies with the Report button under each reply.

2. A chat in BM with a helpful answer (for example "Tolong tulis mesej minta cuti esok")
3. Talk screen while the AI is replying
4. A photo question about a receipt or notice, with the answer
5. "Check your phone" recommendation screen
6. Settings → Privacy dialog

Don't show other apps' logos or notifications in screenshots, and don't add claims like "No. 1" or "Best" to them; Play's metadata policy rejects those.
